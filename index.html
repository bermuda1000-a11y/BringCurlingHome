A place to curl

A place for the community

Curl Ayrshire is developing a new four-sheet curling facility at Somerset Park in Ayr.

But this is about more than curling. The proposed facility will provide spaces all year round for community groups, meetings, activities and events, alongside a modern home for the sport.

In addition, during the summer months, when the ice is removed, the main floor will provide a large, flexible space for conferences, events and other community activities.
