Community Use

A Curl Ayrshire Charity project to create a community facility with curling at its heart.

A Facility for Everyone

Curl Ayrshire Charity (CAC) is developing a new facility at Somerset Park, Ayr, with curling at its heart. But the benefits will extend far beyond the ice. Our vision is to create a welcoming place where people of all ages and abilities can take part, meet others and feel part of their community.

Who Will Benefit?

  • Children and young people — opportunities to try curling, develop skills and discover a new sport.
  • Families — a place to enjoy activities together and share experiences across generations.
  • Older adults — opportunities to stay active, maintain friendships and remain connected.
  • People with disabilities — an accessible facility designed to encourage participation.
  • Clubs, schools and community groups — space for sport, meetings and activities.
  • The wider community — volunteering, competitions, events and opportunities to meet people.

A Place to Meet and Belong

A curling centre can be much more than somewhere to play sport. Social spaces will give people the opportunity to enjoy refreshments, catch up with friends, meet new people and spend time together.

Whether taking part in curling, watching a match, attending a community event or simply coming in for a chat, everyone should feel welcome.

We want the centre to help tackle social isolation, build friendships and strengthen community life across Ayrshire.

Through this project, Curl Ayrshire Charity aims to create a lasting community asset for Ayrshire, bringing people together through sport, social activities and shared experiences.

Curling at its heart. Community at its core. A place for everyone.
