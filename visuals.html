Bringing the vision to life

A project of this size requires professional expertise and careful planning. Curl Ayrshire Charity has engaged a team of experienced consultants to develop the proposals for the new curling facility.

Our design team includes Denham Youd Architects, based in Ayr, and Clancy Consulting, civil engineers based in Prestwick. We have also engaged Harley Haddow to prepare an energy report and Thompson Gray as our cost consultants.

Their work will help us develop the building design, assess its energy performance and establish the costs of delivering the project. These services are required to prepare the plans and supporting documentation for South Ayrshire Council.

A pre-app planning application has been submitted, and we are awaiting the outcome.
