Sustainability

Responsible by design. Built for the future.

Designed for the Future

Curl Ayrshire Charity wants to create a modern, energy-efficient facility that is responsible both environmentally and financially.

Environmental Responsibility

The proposed building will incorporate measures designed to reduce energy consumption and operating costs, including:

  • High levels of insulation and efficient refrigeration.
  • Modern heating, lighting and ice-management systems.
  • Solar panels and battery storage to help meet energy needs.
  • Building controls to monitor and manage energy use.

Our aim is to reduce the facility's environmental impact while creating a comfortable, welcoming place for sport and community activities.

Built to Last

Sustainability also means ensuring that the facility can operate successfully for generations to come. Curl Ayrshire Charity is developing a business plan that supports long-term financial viability, with income from curling, other activities and wider use of the centre.

We want to build a facility that serves its community today while protecting its future.

Responsible by design. Sustainable in operation. Built for generations to come.
